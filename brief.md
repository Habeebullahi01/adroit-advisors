# Website Design Brief: Adroit Advisors

## About the Company
Adroit Advisors is an ICAN-licensed chartered accountancy firm headquartered in Victoria Island, Lagos. They offer a full suite of professional financial services — Audit & Assurance, Tax, Business Advisory, Forensic Accounting, and Big Data Analytics — to businesses across Nigeria. Their Victoria Island address and multi-disciplinary service range signal a firm operating at the top end of the Nigerian professional services market, and their positioning should reflect that.

## Target Audience
Senior decision-makers at Nigerian businesses — CEOs, CFOs, Finance Directors, and business owners — who need a trusted, credentialed firm to handle high-stakes financial work. They are evaluating firms on reputation, expertise, and credibility before making contact. Trust signals matter more than flashy design.

## Website Goal
Primary goal: generate qualified inbound enquiries via the Request for Proposal form.
Secondary goal: establish credibility and authority before a prospect picks up the phone or sends an email.
The #1 action a visitor should take: submit a proposal request.

## Design Style & Mood
Premium, authoritative, and precise. The feeling should be closest to a top-tier professional services firm — think Big Four adjacency without mimicking them. Clean and restrained, with generous whitespace, sharp typography, and no visual clutter. Confident without being cold. The design should communicate: "We are serious people who do serious work."

References in spirit (not to copy): Deloitte, KPMG Nigeria, Andersen Nigeria — firms that signal premium through restraint, not decoration.

## Color Palette
Dominant: Deep navy blue (#0A1F44 or closest brand match) — used on backgrounds, headers, nav, and large surfaces
Accent: Light/medium blue (brand secondary) — used sparingly on CTAs, hover states, dividers, and key highlight moments
Supporting: White and light grey for content backgrounds and breathing room
Guiding principle: Navy carries the authority. Light blue is the signal — use it only where you want the eye to land.

## Typography Direction
Serif or high-quality transitional sans-serif for headings — conveys expertise and gravitas. Clean, readable sans-serif for body copy. Generous line spacing. No decorative or display fonts. Think: GT Sectra, Playfair Display, or similar for headings; Inter or DM Sans for body.

## Imagery & Visual Elements
Professional photography or high-quality stock: boardroom settings, financial documents, collaborative working scenes. Nigerian/Lagos context preferred where available. No generic clipart or cartoon illustrations. Subtle geometric or grid-based background textures in dark sections — financial precision aesthetic. Icons for service categories should be minimal and line-based, not colorful or playful.

## Key Content to Highlight
- ICAN licensing — the credibility anchor; lead with it
- Full-service coverage across Audit, Tax, Advisory, and Forensic — breadth signals stability
- Victoria Island location — location carries weight for the target audience
- "Request a Proposal" as the single, unambiguous next step throughout the site
- Forensic Accounting and Fraud Investigation as a trust-building differentiator (rare specialisation)
- Insights/blog content to establish thought leadership

## Inspiration References
None provided by client. Design direction inferred from Victoria Island positioning, ICAN licensing, and premium professional services market. Aim for restrained authority in the vein of top-tier Nigerian and international advisory firms.

## Additional Notes
- The current site is built on WordPress/Elementor and is visually weak — it does not match the firm's actual market position
- Navigation is currently 3 levels deep and confusing — flatten to two levels maximum; use a clean mega-menu for Services, not a nested dropdown
- The current "Why Choose Us" copy is generic and should be replaced entirely with specific, credibility-led content
- The "How We Work" section on the current site is broken/garbled — rebuild as a clean numbered 3-step sequence: Personal Interview → Engagement → Plan & Maintenance
- Web3/blockchain audit capability exists but is not to be featured prominently unless raised by the client post-engagement
- Single CTA throughout: Request a Proposal — do not repeat the current mistake of competing CTAs

### Homepage Section-by-Section Design Direction

**Nav:** Logo left, main nav links right, single "Request a Proposal" button as a filled accent-colour pill. Services triggers a mega-menu. Max two levels.

**Hero:** Full-width, dark navy background. Large serif heading — their existing line ("The financial world has changed...") is usable, refine it. One subheading sentence. One CTA button only. No competing links.

**Services Grid:** 5 equal-weight cards — Audit & Assurance, Tax Services, Business Advisory, Forensic Accounting, Big Data Analytics. Each card: minimal line icon, service name, one-sentence description, arrow link to detail page. Light background, clean grid layout.

**About Strip:** Short paragraph leading with ICAN licensing and Victoria Island base. One strong credential or stat if available. Link to full About page. Do not use bullet points here — prose only.

**How We Work:** Horizontal numbered sequence — 1. Personal Interview, 2. Engagement, 3. Plan & Maintenance. Clean, simple, no decorative elements.

**CTA Banner:** Full-width dark navy section. Single bold headline. Single "Request a Proposal" button. Nothing else on this section.

**Footer:** Services list (left column), contact details (middle), social links + ICAN badge (right). Dark background. Clean grid.

### Guiding Design Principle
Every page should feel like it costs more than what they're currently charging. The gap between their actual market position (Victoria Island, ICAN-licensed, forensic capability) and their current website is the pitch. The sample site just needs to close that gap visually.
